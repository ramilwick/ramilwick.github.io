Suwasetha EMR Backup is an owner-operated utility being configured to save encrypted database backups from Suwasetha Medical Centre's clinical system into a private Google Drive folder.

Read our privacy policy →

This information website is public. It does not host patient records, let patients sign in, or provide medical consultations. The automated backup service is being prepared and is not represented here as already active.

Purpose and process

How the backup utility works

01

Create a consistent database export

A dedicated cloud backup job exports a PostgreSQL snapshot of the clinic database for disaster recovery.

02

Encrypt before upload

The export is protected using AES-256-GCM encryption before transfer to Google Drive. The decryption key is held separately.

03

Store privately on Google Drive

The utility uploads encrypted archives to a private folder authorized by the Google account owner, for use in a controlled recovery process.

Google authorization

Limited access to Google Drive

The utility requests the drive.file permission. This permits access to files the application creates or files explicitly authorized by the account holder; it does not request blanket permission to browse all existing Google Drive files.

Google Drive is used as an encrypted backup destination, not as the clinic's live patient database. Read how Google account information, backup archives, and credentials are handled in our privacy policy.
